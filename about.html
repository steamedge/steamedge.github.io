OUR STORY

A simple question:
What could a razor become?

SteamEdge began with an opportunity to rethink a product people already know. Instead of treating the razor as only a handle and blade, the concept explores how a steam feature could become part of the tool itself.

That idea shaped the direction of the product: a rechargeable handle, a pivoting razor head, an integrated refillable steam system, and a coordinated range of accessories. The design pairs a modern dark finish with a straightforward layout so the product feels intentional from the first look to everyday use.

SteamEdge is still a product concept. Our focus is to develop the idea, communicate the design clearly, and continue refining the experience as the product takes shape.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our design direction.

01

Function first

Every feature should have a reason to be there and contribute to the overall product experience.

02

03

A clean shape, focused controls, and a dark, confident visual identity guide the SteamEdge look.
