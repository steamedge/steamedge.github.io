PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Meet SteamEdge.

A razor concept designed to bring a steam feature, a pivoting head, and a rechargeable handle together in one product.

STEAMEDGE / FRONT VIEW

01 / THE PRODUCT

A familiar tool,
rethought.

SteamEdge is a modern razor concept centered on a rechargeable handle with a built-in, refillable steam system. The product combines the main razor body and its controls with a pivoting head, creating one connected design rather than a traditional razor with a separate preparation step.

The handle is shaped to give the product a streamlined profile while keeping its primary controls visible and accessible. At the top, the razor head connects to the handle and is designed to pivot as the user moves across the shaving area. The overall look uses a dark, metallic finish with contrasting black components to give SteamEdge a clean, technical style.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

One connected system.

The SteamEdge concept brings the steam function into the razor itself. These steps describe the intended product experience.

01

Prepare

Fill the integrated, refillable steam reservoir according to the final product instructions.

02

Power on

Use the handle controls to activate the product's powered functions.

03

Shave

As the razor is used, the concept is designed to direct steam below the blade area while the head pivots with movement.

04

Recharge & care

Recharge the handle with its charging cable and keep the razor and accessories clean between uses.

Product-concept note: final operating instructions, heating behavior, reservoir capacity, and performance specifications should be confirmed through product testing before commercial use.

03 / DESIGN DETAILS

Every part has
a place.

A

Pivoting razor head

The replaceable head is a defining part of the product and is designed to move with the handle during use.

B

Streamlined handle

The handle narrows through the neck and widens into a comfortable-looking grip, with the main controls positioned on the front.

C

Charging connection

A dedicated cable supports the rechargeable-handle concept and gives the product a clear place in a daily routine.
